CITIES IN INDIA

  1. Andhra Pradesh
    • Visakhapatnam
      • Visakhapatnam is a major port city and industrial hub in Andhra Pradesh, known for its beaches and steel plant.
    • Vijayawada
      • Vijayawada is a vibrant commercial and cultural hub in Andhra Pradesh, situated on the banks of the Krishna River.
    • Guntur
      • Guntur is famous for its chilli market and agriculture, and is one of the largest cities in Andhra Pradesh.
    • Tirupati
      • Tirupati is a holy city known for the Sri Venkateswara Temple, one of the most visited pilgrimage centers in India.
    • Rajahmundry
      • Rajahmundry is the cultural capital of Andhra Pradesh, situated on the banks of the sacred Godavari River.
  2. Telangana
    • Hyderabad
      • Hyderabad is the capital city, famous for Charminar, Biryani and IT industry.
    • Warangal
      • Warangal is known for its historical Kakatiya temples and rich cultural heritage.
    • Nizamabad
      • Nizamabad is an agricultural hub famous for turmeric and rice production.
    • Karimnagar
      • Karimnagar is known for its granite industry and scenic countryside.
    • Khammam
      • Khammam is famous for its temples and is located near the Godavari River.
  3. Karnataka
    • Bengaluru
      • Bengaluru is the IT capital of India, known as Silicon Valley of India.
    • Mysuru
      • Mysuru is famous for Mysore Palace and Dasara festival.
    • Mangaluru
      • Mangaluru is a major port city known for its beaches and seafood.
    • Hublu
      • Hubli is an important commercial and industrial center in North Karnataka.
    • Belagavi
      • Belagavi is known for its historical forts and sugarcane production.
  4. Tamil Nadu
    • Chennai
      • Chennai is the capital city, famous for Marina Beach and automobile industry.
    • Coimbatore
      • Coimbatore is known as Manchester of South India for its textile industry.
    • Madurai
      • Madurai is famous for Meenakshi Temple and rich Tamil culture.
    • Salem
      • Salem is known for steel plants and mango production.
    • Tiruchirappalli
      • Trichy is famous for Rockfort Temple and educational institutions.
  5. Kerala
    • Thiruvananthapuram
      • Thiruvananthapuram is the capital city, known for Padmanabhaswamy Temple.
    • Kochi
      • Kochi is a major port city and commercial hub of Kerala.
    • Kozhikode
      • Kozhikode is famous for its beaches and historical trade connections.
    • Thrissur
      • Thrissur is known as Cultural Capital of Kerala for its festivals.
    • Alappuzha
      • Alappuzha is famous for backwaters and houseboat tourism.
  6. Maharashtra
    • Mumbai
      • Mumbai is the financial capital of India and Bollywood hub.
    • Pune
      • Pune is known as educational and IT hub of Maharashtra.
    • Nagpur
      • Nagpur is famous for oranges and is the geographical center of India.
    • Nashik
      • Nashik is known for wine production and Kumbh Mela.
    • Aurangabad
      • Aurangabad is famous for Ajanta and Ellora caves.
  7. Gujarat
    • Ahmedabad
      • Ahmedabad is the largest city in Gujarat, known for textiles and business.
    • Surat
      • Surat is famous for diamond cutting and textile industry.
    • Vadodara
      • Vadodara is known for its palaces and cultural heritage.
    • Rajkot
      • Rajkot is an important industrial and commercial center.
    • Bhuj
      • Bhuj is famous for handicrafts and is located near Rann of Kutch.
